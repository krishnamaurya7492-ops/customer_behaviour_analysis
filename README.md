# Customer behaviour analysis
End-to-end Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics project using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI to clean, analyze, and visualize 3,900 customer transactions and uncover actionable business insights.

## Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Data Analytics project** focused on analyzing customer shopping behavior and identifying meaningful business insights from transactional data.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow — from **data loading and cleaning in Python** to **SQL-based business analysis**, followed by an interactive **Power BI dashboard**, a detailed project report, and a presentation created using **Gamma**.

The objective is to understand customer spending patterns, product performance, subscription behavior, customer segments, discounts, shipping preferences, and age-wise revenue contribution.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records** with **18 columns** covering:

- Customer demographics
- Age and gender
- Products and categories
- Purchase amounts
- Location, size, color and season
- Review ratings
- Subscription status
- Shipping type
- Discount and promotional activity
- Previous purchases
- Payment method
- Purchase frequency

The dataset was first explored and cleaned before being loaded into SQL for further analysis.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading

The dataset was loaded into Python using **Pandas** and initially inspected to understand its structure, columns, data types, and overall quality.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

EDA was performed using functions such as:

- `df.info()`
- `df.describe()`
- `df.isnull().sum()`

This helped identify data types, missing values, categorical variables, and basic statistical characteristics.

### 3. Data Cleaning & Transformation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis by:

- Handling missing values in the `Review Rating` column
- Imputing missing ratings using category-level median values
- Standardizing column names using snake_case
- Creating an `age_group` feature
- Converting purchase frequency into `purchase_frequency_days`
- Checking redundancy between discount and promo-code fields
- Removing the redundant `promo_code_used` column

### 4. SQL Analysis

The cleaned dataset was loaded into **SQL Server** for structured business analysis.

SQL queries were used to investigate questions such as:

- Revenue generated by gender
- High-spending customers using discounts
- Top-rated products
- Average purchase amount by shipping type
- Subscribers vs. non-subscribers
- Products with high discount dependency
- Customer segmentation
- Top products within each category
- Repeat buyers and subscription behavior
- Revenue contribution by age group

### 5. Power BI Dashboard

The analytical results were brought into **Power BI** to create an interactive customer behavior dashboard.

The dashboard includes:

- Total number of customers
- Average purchase amount
- Average review rating
- Subscription status
- Revenue by category
- Sales by category
- Revenue by age group
- Sales by age group
- Interactive filters for customer and purchase attributes

### 6. Reporting & Presentation

The final findings were documented in a structured project report and converted into a presentation using **Gamma** to communicate the project, insights, and recommendations effectively.

## Key Results

The analysis helped identify several useful business insights:

- The dataset contains **3,900 customer transactions**.
- Clothing generated the highest revenue among the major product categories.
- A significant portion of customers falls into the **Loyal** customer segment.
- Subscription customers can be compared with non-subscribers based on spending and revenue contribution.
- Express shipping showed a slightly higher average purchase amount than standard shipping.
- Certain products showed a high dependence on discounts.
- Age-group analysis highlighted differences in revenue contribution across customer segments.
- Product-level analysis helped identify frequently purchased and highly rated products.

These findings can support decisions around **customer retention, subscription growth, discount strategy, product promotion, and targeted marketing**.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Encourage more customers to join subscription programs through exclusive benefits.
- Introduce loyalty initiatives for repeat customers.
- Review discount strategies to balance sales growth with profitability.
- Promote highly rated and frequently purchased products.
- Use customer age groups and purchasing behavior for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Analyze shipping preferences to optimize customer experience.
